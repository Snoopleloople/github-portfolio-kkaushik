AI Study Assistant Documentation
Project Overview
The AI Study Assistant is a web application designed to help students learn more effectively by interacting with their course materials.

Students can upload lecture notes, study guides, or PDFs and use artificial intelligence to ask questions, generate summaries, create flashcards, and prepare for exams.

Features:

Document Upload

*Upload PDF lecture notes
*Upload study guides
*Store and process course materials

AI Question Answering
*Ask questions about uploaded documents
*Receive context-aware answers
*Improve comprehension of difficult topics

Automatic Summarization
*Generate concise summaries
*Highlight key concepts
*Create review sheets

Flashcard Generator
*Convert notes into study flashcards
*Support self-testing and memorization

Quiz Generation
*Create practice questions
*Generate multiple-choice quizzes
*Assist with exam preparation

Technologies Used:
